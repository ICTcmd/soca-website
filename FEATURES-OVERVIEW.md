# 🎯 SOCA Website Features Overview

## 📺 Video & Presentation Section

### Video Player
- HTML5 video player for local video file
- 16:9 aspect ratio
- Standard video controls (play, pause, volume, fullscreen)
- No YouTube required - plays directly from your files

### Presentation Slide Viewer (NEW! ⭐)
The same 120 PowerPoint slides shown during the Mayor's speech, now interactive!

#### Main Features:
- **📊 Slide Display**: Large, clear view of current slide
- **🔢 Counter**: Shows "15 / 120" (current / total)
- **📈 Progress Bar**: Visual indicator of position in presentation
- **⬅️ ➡️ Navigation**: Previous/Next arrow buttons

#### Auto-Play Controls:
- **▶️ Play/Pause**: Start/stop automatic slideshow
- **⚡ Speed Selector**: 
  - Fast (2 seconds)
  - Normal (3 seconds)
  - Moderate (4 seconds) - default
  - Slow (5 seconds)
- **🔄 Loop**: Automatically returns to slide 1 after slide 120

#### Grid View:
- **🗂️ Thumbnail Gallery**: See all 120 slides at once
- **🖱️ Click to Jump**: Instantly go to any slide
- **💾 Lazy Loading**: Fast performance, loads thumbnails as needed
- **✨ Active Highlight**: Current slide highlighted in orange

#### Keyboard Shortcuts:
- **← →** Arrow keys: Navigate slides
- **Space Bar**: Play/Pause auto-play
- **Home**: Jump to first slide (slide 1)
- **End**: Jump to last slide (slide 120)

#### Performance Optimized:
- Lazy loading images
- Smooth transitions
- Works with 120+ slides without lag
- Mobile-friendly touch controls
