State of the City Address 2026 • Bago City Hall

To our Vice Mayor, the honorable members of the Sangguniang Panlungsod, our department heads, partners from the national government, civil society, the private sector, dear students and above all, sa inyo tanan nga mga pinalangga ko nga kasimanwa: Maayong Hapon.

Before I speak of where we are today, allow me to honor those who came before us. Every city is shaped not by one leader alone, but by generations of men and women who chose to serve.

To our past Local Chief Executives, former public officials, dedicated civil servants, and every individual who helped build the foundations of this beloved city, we offer our profound gratitude.

"Public office is not ownership. It is stewardship. Leadership is never measured by the title we bear, but by the burdens we are willing to carry for others."

A Trust from the People

Today is not merely a report of what has been accomplished over the past year. It is an accounting of a trust. One year ago, you entrusted us with something far more valuable than authority. You entrusted us with your hopes.

The hope of every mother wishing for affordable healthcare. The hope of every farmer hoping for a better harvest, every student daring to believe that education can change the trajectory of their life. Every worker, every entrepreneur, every senior citizen, every child who deserves a government that remembers their name, their longings, their dreams, their aspirations—isa ka gobyerno nga ga ulikid, ga tatap kag ga amuma saila.

"What does the Lord require of us? To act justly, to love mercy, and to walk humbly with our God." — Micah 6:8

May Gugma nga Panghimanwa

In Bago, we know that service is not enough. It must be done with heart. And that heart is what we call: MAY GUGMA NGA PANGHIMANWA.

Panghimanwa means government that feels. Government that cares. Government that serves like family. A love that kneels before it leads. A love that must be felt, not just heard.

That love is what guides us in everything we do under our framework: ASENSO BAGO.

A - Agriculture and Food Security

Before our city comes alive each morning, someone is already awake. A farmer walks into the fields before sunrise. A fisherfolk heads out to the water, never fully certain what the day will bring.

This year, we gave our biggest seed support yet. We distributed 34,200 bags of certified and hybrid palay seeds worth ₱44.51 Million. 10,263 rice farmers across 23 barangays received direct support to increase yield and secure harvest.

We expanded beyond rice to secure livelihoods, supporting 1,250 farmers with banana, coffee, and vegetables. We established HAPAG gardens in 6 barangays and 6 schools, and continued assisting our fisherfolk through distribution of fiberglass boats, tilapia fingerlings, fishing equipment, and establishment of artificial reefs.

E - Education: The Future We Choose Today

I have always believed that one of the greatest investments any government can make is education. Because roads may connect communities. Buildings may serve generations. But education changes destinies.

For Bago City College, we allocated a big chunk of the 20 percent Development Fund for the construction of a four-story building that will house additional classrooms, laboratories, and a student dormitory—ensuring that students coming from distant barangays have a safe and affordable place to stay while pursuing their education.

"The greatest inheritance we can leave our children is not land or wealth. It is the knowledge they gain and the opportunities they can carry with them wherever life takes them."

Bago City College recorded a total enrollment of 6,162 students—the biggest in its recorded history. Last AY 2025-2026, we produced 1,157 graduates from its 4 program offerings.

Health: Healing Should Never Be Far From Home

We have always believed that healthcare should never be a privilege reserved for a few. It is a basic right that every Bagonhon deserves.

Through the support of our City Council and our healthcare team, we expanded our services. We added a Dialysis Center through Hemoserve, a larger OPD, and hired additional doctors and nurses. We added ultrasound and ECG services so more people can access quality healthcare.

Through the First Lady's Lab for All Program, nearly 2,000 Bagonhons received free laboratory services and medical consultations.

Government Closer to the People

Instead of asking people to travel to City Hall, we asked ourselves: "Paano kita makakadto sa ila?"

That simple question gave birth to many of the programs we are most proud of today. We established satellite offices in Ma-ao, bringing the services of the City Social Welfare and Development Office, the PAO sa Ma-ao, and the SSS E-Center closer to thousands of residents.

Through our Serbisyo sa Barangay Caravan, government literally came to our communities. Thousands of clients were served through medical consultations, legal assistance, civil registry services, social welfare, PhilHealth registration, employment assistance, and many other frontline government services.

Infrastructure and Development

From July 2025 to June 2026, your City Government invested in infrastructure that connects communities, protects families, and improves basic services in every barangay.

Through the Office of the City Engineer, we completed 22 infrastructure projects worth ₱36.9 million that directly improved health, education, tourism, and public service facilities.

We also built 10 reinforced concrete box culverts and an ongoing 40-meter hanging bridge to strengthen flood resilience and ensure that no community is cut off during calamities.

"These roads, lights, water systems, and public facilities are more than projects. They are pathways to schools, to hospitals, to markets, and to each other."

The Road Ahead

While we celebrate every road we have opened, there are still communities waiting to be connected. While we celebrate every child we have helped send to school, there are still dreams waiting for their chance.

We envision a Bago where every farmer enjoys secure harvests and sustainable livelihoods, where every learner has the opportunity to pursue education regardless of circumstance, where quality healthcare is within the reach of every family, where entrepreneurs and workers find opportunities to grow, and where our environment is protected for generations yet to come.

Because progress is never measured only by the number of roads we pave, buildings we construct, or programs we launch. True progress is measured by the confidence of a father who can provide for his family, the peace of mind of a senior citizen who knows government will never forget them, the hope of a young student who believes education can change the course of life, and the dignity of every Bagonhon who feels seen, heard, and valued.

"To serve Bago is to serve with love. And with love, there is progress. With love, there is healing. With love, there is hope."

Madamo guid nga Salamat! Sa gihapon, padayon kita sa panghimanwa nga puno gid sang gugma. Sa aton paghugpong, sa aton pagbuligay, kag sa aton hugot nga pag-ampo sa Ginoo, padayon kita sa pag-asenso! Pinalangga nga Siyudad sang Bago!
