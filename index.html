State of the City Address 2026 • Bago City Hall

Sa inyo tanan nga mga pinalangga ko nga kasimanwa: Maayong Hapon.

"Public office is not ownership. It is stewardship. Leadership is never measured by the title we bear, but by the burdens we are willing to carry for others."

May Gugma nga Panghimanwa

One year ago, you entrusted us with your hopes. The hope of every mother wishing for affordable healthcare, every farmer hoping for a better harvest, every student believing that education can change their life.

In Bago, service must be done with heart. That heart is what we call: MAY GUGMA NGA PANGHIMANWA—government that feels, cares, and serves like family. This is the foundation of ASENSO BAGO.

Key Accomplishments

Agriculture & Food Security
Distributed 34,200 bags of certified seeds worth ₱44.51 Million to 10,263 rice farmers across 23 barangays. Supported 1,250 farmers with high-value crops, established HAPAG gardens, and assisted fisherfolk with equipment and artificial reefs.

Education
Bago City College achieved record enrollment of 6,162 students—the largest in history—and produced 1,157 graduates. Allocated major funding for a four-story building with classrooms, laboratories, and student dormitory to ensure distance is never a barrier to education.

Healthcare
Expanded hospital services with Dialysis Center, larger OPD, additional doctors and nurses. Through First Lady's Lab for All, served nearly 2,000 Bagonhons with free laboratory services. Launched See Bright Bago, Smile Bright Bago, and distributed wheelchairs, canes, and assistive devices to 333 residents.

"Healthcare should never be a privilege. It is a basic right that every Bagonhon deserves."

Infrastructure & Services
Completed 22 infrastructure projects worth ₱36.9 million. Brought government services closer through satellite offices in Ma-ao, Serbisyo sa Barangay Caravan reaching thousands, and Project Night Care for after-hours health assistance.

Economic Development
Welcomed major investments: Imperial Appliance Plaza, Watsons, Jollibee, Dunkin' Donuts, Robinson's Supermarket, and a landmark 150-Megawatt Wind Power Project. Held 5 Job Fairs with 16,794 vacancies, resulting in 45 immediate hires.

Social Programs
Held Kasalan Mass Wedding 2025 for 224 couples and Mass Wedding 2026 for 91 couples with free CENOMAR processing. Expanded senior citizen local pension by 300% and issued 12,237 Digital Senior PVC IDs. Served 3,950 residents through Idangup kay Mayora Facebook Page.

Financial Stewardship

Generated ₱1.95 Billion in total revenue. Invested ₱75.34 Million in Social Services, ₱34.83 Million in Economic Services, ₱92.39 Million for Roads, and ₱10.73 Million for Water. Recognized by DILG as 2025 Good Financial Housekeeping Passer.

"Every peso we collect is a peso we return to you in the form of service, with love."

The Road Ahead

True progress is measured by the confidence of a father who can provide for his family, the peace of mind of a senior citizen who knows government will never forget them, the hope of a young student who believes education can change their life, and the dignity of every Bagonhon who feels seen, heard, and valued.

We will continue investing in agriculture, education, healthcare, employment, infrastructure, and environmental protection—not as isolated projects, but as interconnected investments that uplift every Bagonhon.

"To serve Bago is to serve with love. And with love, there is progress. With love, there is healing. With love, there is hope."

Madamo guid nga Salamat! Padayon kita sa panghimanwa nga puno gid sang gugma. Pinalangga nga Siyudad sang Bago!
