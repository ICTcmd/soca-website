SOCA 2026 Highlights

"Public office is not ownership. It is stewardship. Leadership is never measured by the title we bear, but by the burdens we are willing to carry for others."

May Gugma nga Panghimanwa

One year ago, you entrusted us with your hopes—the hope of every mother wishing for affordable healthcare, every farmer hoping for a better harvest, every student believing that education can change their life.

In Bago, service must be done with heart. That heart is what we call: MAY GUGMA NGA PANGHIMANWA—government that feels, cares, and serves like family. This is the foundation of ASENSO BAGO.

Key Accomplishments

Agriculture & Food Security
Distributed 34,200 bags of certified seeds worth ₱44.51 Million to 10,263 rice farmers across 23 barangays. Supported 1,250 farmers with high-value crops, established HAPAG gardens in 6 barangays and 6 schools, and assisted fisherfolk with equipment and artificial reefs.

Education
Bago City College achieved record enrollment of 6,162 students—the largest in history—and produced 1,157 graduates. Allocated major funding for a four-story building with classrooms, laboratories, and student dormitory. Launched Iskolar Bago Program, serving 850 scholars with ₱5,000 annual assistance.

Healthcare
Expanded hospital services with Dialysis Center, larger OPD, additional doctors and nurses. Through First Lady's Lab for All, served nearly 2,000 Bagonhons with free laboratory services. Launched See Bright Bago (71 beneficiaries), Smile Bright Bago (free dental services), and distributed 189 wheelchairs, 86 canes, and 58 specialized wheelchairs through Wheels of Love.

"Healthcare should never be a privilege. It is a basic right that every Bagonhon deserves."

Infrastructure & Services
Completed 22 infrastructure projects worth ₱36.9 million. Built footbridges, health centers, playgrounds, and public facilities. Brought government services closer through satellite offices in Ma-ao, Serbisyo sa Barangay Caravan reaching 6,000+ residents in 11 barangays, and Project Night Care for after-hours health assistance.

Economic Development
Welcomed major investments: Imperial Appliance Plaza, Watsons, Jollibee, Dunkin' Donuts, Robinson's Supermarket, and the landmark 150-Megawatt Wind Power Project covering six barangays. Held 5 Job Fairs with 96 companies offering 16,794 vacancies, resulting in 45 immediate hires.

Social Programs
Held Kasalan Mass Wedding 2025 for 224 couples and Mass Wedding 2026 for 91 couples with free CENOMAR processing. Expanded senior citizen local pension by 300% and issued 12,237 Digital Senior PVC IDs. Served 3,950 residents through Idangup kay Mayora Facebook Page. PhilHealth Advocates registered 28,874 Bagonhons.

Tourism & Culture
Invested ₱32.3 Million in tourism, reaching over 100,000 locals and visitors. Launched OTOP Hub at Bantayan Park, celebrated the 60th Charter Anniversary with Bugana Festival, Moda kag Monyeka, and Paskwa sa Bago. Showcased Bago culture in Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia.

Financial Stewardship

Generated ₱1.95 Billion in total revenue. Invested ₱75.34 Million in Social Services, ₱34.83 Million in Economic Services, ₱92.39 Million for Roads, and ₱10.73 Million for Water. Recognized by DILG as 2025 Good Financial Housekeeping Passer.

"Every peso we collect is a peso we return to you in the form of service, with love."

Flagship Programs Under ASENSO BAGO

For Education & Youth: Iskolar Bago (850 scholars annually), Atleta Bago (sports development), See Bright Bago (free eyeglasses), Cine Bago (free community film screenings)

For Health & Wellness: LaBago (laboratory services), Give Blood Bago (3,855 blood bags collected), Smile Bright Bago (dental care), Botica Bago (free medicines worth ₱20.4M), Heart Warriors Bago (8 cardiac surgeries), Breathe Free Bago (free nebulizers), Wheels of Love (mobility devices)

For Infrastructure: Tubig Bago (40,000 metric tons water delivered), Sanag Bago (187 households energized), Build Bago (youth hubs, libraries, playgrounds, Senior Citizens Center)

For Livelihood & Jobs: Trabaho Bago (job fairs), Kari sa Bago (business investments), TESDA scholarships (567 Bagonhons trained)

The Road Ahead

True progress is measured by the confidence of a father who can provide for his family, the peace of mind of a senior citizen who knows government will never forget them, the hope of a young student who believes education can change their life, and the dignity of every Bagonhon who feels seen, heard, and valued.

We will continue investing in modern agriculture, education, healthcare, employment, infrastructure, tourism, and environmental protection—not as isolated projects, but as interconnected investments that uplift every Bagonhon and every barangay.

"To serve Bago is to serve with love. And with love, there is progress. With love, there is healing. With love, there is hope."

Madamo guid nga Salamat! Padayon kita sa panghimanwa nga puno gid sang gugma. Pinalangga nga Siyudad sang Bago!
